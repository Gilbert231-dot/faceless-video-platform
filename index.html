How it works

A fully automated content pipeline, from story to published video.

1

Pick a story

The app pulls popular Reddit stories and picks one at random, never repeating a story you've already used.

2

AI rewrites the script

A large language model rewrites the story into a first-person, hook-first narration tuned for short-form video.

3

Voiceover + captions

A premium AI voice narrates the story while word-synced captions are burned into the video over cinematic background footage.

4

Auto-post on schedule

The finished video is uploaded and scheduled to go public on your channels at the best posting times — hands-free.
