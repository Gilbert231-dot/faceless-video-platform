Faceless Video Creator poster Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Faceless Video Creator poster respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it to provide our video creation service. In this policy, "we", "our" and "us" refer to Faceless Video Creator poster.

1. Information We Collect

  • TikTok Authentication Data: When you connect your TikTok account, we receive the basic profile information TikTok makes available through its official Login Kit: your display name, your avatar image and a TikTok account identifier. We use it solely to identify the account we publish to. We never receive your TikTok password or your email address.
  • User-Provided Content: We collect the text stories you submit (or select from the curated Reddit story folders) to generate video scripts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To rewrite and transform user-submitted stories into original AI-generated scripts.
  • To convert these scripts into voiceovers using Text-to-Speech (TTS) services.
  • To compile, edit, and render the final video with dynamic visuals and subtitles.
  • To upload the finalized video directly to your connected TikTok account via the official TikTok API.

3. Data Storage & Security

Our storage is deliberately minimal. Videos, voiceovers and audio files are created on the fly, uploaded to TikTok at your request, and deleted from our servers once processing is complete. We do not keep a library of your finished videos, audio files or images.

The only things we keep are what we need to operate the service: the identifiers of the videos we published for you, the status of scheduled and in-progress jobs, and the story text used to generate each narration.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We share minimal necessary data with trusted third-party APIs solely to operate the service:

  • TikTok API: To authenticate you and publish your videos.
  • AI/LLM Services: To rewrite and enhance your submitted text.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS) Providers: To generate natural-sounding voiceovers.
  • Stock Media APIs: To fetch background footage and images (if applicable).

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with advertisers, marketers, or unauthorized third parties.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data we hold. Since we do not keep your videos or audio, a deletion request applies to the link between our service and your TikTok account (your TikTok account identifier) and to the story text and job records we store. To request deletion, contact us using the email below and we will remove them.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
